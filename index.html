About Above & Beyond

Run by Carissa Sloan — your neighbor on Garfield Street.

Above & Beyond Hair Design is independently owned and operated right in the heart of Downtown Laramie. Carissa built the salon around one idea: every client, whether they're in for a quick trim or a full wedding-day transformation, gets the same relaxed, unhurried attention.

Clients of every age and background are welcome here — from a first kids' haircut to color corrections, deep conditioning treatments, and lash and skin services. If you don't see exactly what you need, a text or call is always the fastest way to ask.

— Carissa Sloan, Owner & Stylist
